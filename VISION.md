## 🎯 **Core Vision**
Create the most flexible, intelligent, and user-friendly mental health tracking platform that combines:
- **Airtable's flexibility** - Customizable views, relationships, and workflows
- **Mental health expertise** - Deep domain knowledge and clinical insights
- **AI-powered intelligence** - Pattern recognition and predictive analytics
- **Beautiful design** - Intuitive, calming, empowering interface

---

## 🚀 **Key Differentiators**

### 1. **Flexible Mental Health Database**
Instead of rigid forms, users build their own mental health "base":
- **Custom Fields**: Track what matters to YOU (sleep, meals, exercise, social interactions, medications, symptoms)
- **Field Types**: Text, Number, Date, Rating, Emotion, Multiple Select, Attachments, Voice Notes
- **Relationships**: Connect moods → activities, symptoms → triggers, medications → side effects
- **Auto-linking**: AI automatically suggests connections

### 2. **Multiple Intelligent Views**
Transform the same data into different perspectives:

#### **📊 Timeline View** (Default)
- Chronological stream of all entries
- Color-coded by mood/wellness score
- Quick-add floating button
- Inline editing
- Expandable cards with full details

#### **📅 Calendar View**
- Month/week/day layouts
- Visual mood heatmap
- Drag-and-drop to reschedule activities
- Recurring entries support
- Integration with external calendars

#### **📈 Analytics View**
- Interactive charts and graphs
- Correlation explorer
- Trend identification
- Predictive insights
- Custom date ranges

#### **🗺️ Journey Map View**
- Visual story of your mental health journey
- Milestone markers
- Progress tracking
- Before/after comparisons
- Shareable with therapist

#### **🎯 Goals & Habits View**
- Habit tracker with streaks
- Goal progress bars
- Completion rates
- Reward system
- Custom reminders

#### **🧩 Insights Board**
- AI-generated pattern cards
- "Things I've noticed" section
- Correlation discoveries
- Personalized recommendations
- Exportable reports

#### **🤝 Collaborative View**
- Share with therapist/support circle
- Controlled permissions (what they see)
- Comment threads
- Care team coordination
- Emergency contacts integration

## 📱 **User Flow Examples**

### **Morning Check-in Flow**
1. Open app → Gentle "Good morning" greeting
2. Today's weather shown → "How are you feeling?"
3. Quick mood slider → "How did you sleep?"
4. Optional: Add voice note about dreams/morning thoughts
5. AI suggests: "Want to log your morning routine?"
6. One-tap add: Coffee ☕ Meditation 🧘 Shower 🚿
7. "Great start! Your wellness score: 72/100 (+5 from yesterday)"

### **Pattern Discovery Flow**
1. User clicks "Insights" tab
2. AI card appears: "New pattern detected!"
3. Visual graph showing: Exercise days = Better mood next day
4. "Would you like to set an exercise reminder?"
5. Quick setup: Days, times, activity type
6. Added to habits tracker automatically

### **Therapist Collaboration Flow**
1. User clicks "Share with therapist"
2. Generates secure share code
3. Selects what to share:
   - ✅ Mood trends (yes)
   - ✅ Journal entries (yes)
   - ❌ Medication details (no - privacy)
   - ✅ Goals progress (yes)
4. Sets duration: "Until next appointment" or "Ongoing"
5. Therapist views read-only dashboard with insights
6. Can leave supportive comments

## 🎨 **Design System**

### **Color Psychology**
- **Primary**: Calming Purple (#8B5CF6) - Trust, creativity
- **Success**: Soft Green (#10B981) - Growth, healing
- **Warning**: Warm Orange (#F59E0B) - Energy, motivation
- **Danger**: Gentle Red (#EF4444) - Urgency without alarm
- **Neutral**: Warm Grays - Comfort, stability

### **Typography**
- **Headings**: Inter (modern, friendly)
- **Body**: -apple-system, system-ui (native, accessible)
- **Data**: JetBrains Mono (clarity for numbers)

### **Spacing System**
- Base unit: 4px
- Scale: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64
- Generous whitespace reduces anxiety
